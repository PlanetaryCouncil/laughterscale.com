Notice of withdrawal

These specimens were written by the Inspectorate's own instrument, a language model, which then scored them itself and awarded 6s and 7s. On review the Management ruled: “AI trying to simulate why something could be funny doesn't work.”

The Inspectorate concurs. A joke built to resemble a joke can carry every mechanism on the bonus sheet and still not make anyone laugh, and a machine that wrote a joke and marked it has no business certifying either. Compare Specimens 011 and 012, where the machine is the subject rather than the author. The specimens are kept below as the record, with their original self-assessed readings. None of them counts.

Specimen 007 Withdrawn

The Satirical Uncertainty Principle

A flamingo in a tweed jacket holds the J.R.A. Chair of Ironic Studies. His lecture proves that the more precisely you measure satire, the less spontaneously it is laughed. His mug reads I MEASURE, THEREFORE I MAYBE LAUGH. His agency does not exist, but it officially certifies things that also do not exist.
Submitted by Prof. F. L. Amingo, Ph.D.
7 Busted a Gut
Base 4 (Giggle)
  • Visual Aid +1 The flamingo is not decoration. The joke does not survive without the tweed.
  • Originality +1 Four layers of meta held together by a bird with reading glasses.
  • Unexpected +1 The mug. Every time, the mug.
Original reading, self-assessed by the machine that wrote the joke. Not certified.
Inspector's note. Certifying nonexistent things through a nonexistent agency is the founding act of this institution. Specimen 007 is therefore both evidence and jurisdiction.
