Specimen 009

Due Diligence

I sent money to a dude in a hot tub in Hawaii to 5× my money. OUTRAGE! SHOCKING! To be fair, he called it a 'donation' and a 'game'.
Submitted by The Management
10 Dead
Base 5 (Laugh)
  • Unexpected +1 'To be fair.' The victim rises to the defence of the man in the hot tub.
  • Relatable +1 Every reader has watched someone do exactly this. Some have watched themselves.
  • Visual Aid +1 The phone on the tripod is marked LIVE. The transaction is being streamed.
  • Slant Rhyme +0.5 Awarded on a grievance from the Labour Union of Joke Interpreters. HAWAII / MONEY upheld: both end a line and land on the same unstressed 'ee'. FAIR / GAME rejected as cited: different vowel, different ending. The union missed the stronger pair: DONATION / GAME share a stressed 'ay', and they are the two words in scare quotes.
  • Audience Amplifier x2 Documented in Exhibit A: seven crying-laughing emoji, an unbroken 'ahhahahahaah', and a second witness reduced to 'Wow'. The reaction is funnier than most jokes, which is the multiplier's entire definition.
Raw reading (5 + 3.5) × 2 = 17. The needle stops at 10; position 11 requires Legendary status.
Inspector's note. The first specimen to exceed the instrument. It does not reach 11, because 11 is conferred, not computed. The Inspectorate further finds that no fraud occurred: the terms were disclosed in full, and they were 'donation' and 'game'. Amended on appeal: raw reading revised from 16 to 17. The needle did not move.
Specimen 001

The Humour Uncertainty Principle

You can know how funny something is, or you can laugh at it — never both at once. The preservation law holds, though. The LOLness isn't destroyed. It just goes latent, and comes back when you least expect it: usually at 3 AM, or in a quiet meeting.
Submitted by The Management
8 Crying Laughing
Base 5 (Laugh)
  • Originality +1 Uncertainty jokes are common. Conservation of LOLness is not.
  • Relatable +1 Everyone has been ambushed by a latent joke in a room where laughing was a bad idea.
  • Unexpected +1 The pivot from uncertainty to conservation is the actual gag.
Inspector's note. The specimen demonstrates its own thesis. It was measurably funnier before we measured it. Per its own preservation law, that LOLness is not gone — it is latent, and it will find you.
Specimen 008

Satire Non-Proliferation Treaty

You can inspect a stockpile of warheads. You cannot inspect a stockpile of jokes that haven't landed yet — because per the uncertainty principle, measuring them disarms them. Measurement constitutes disarmament.
Submitted by Prof. F. L. Amingo, Ph.D., Commanding Officer, Battalion of Militarized Satire
8 Crying Laughing
Base 5 (Laugh)
  • Originality +1 Arms-control law is not a worn path to a punchline, and the treaty language fits the physics exactly.
  • Unexpected +1 The asymmetry does it — warheads hold still to be counted, jokes do not.
  • Visual Aid +1 Pink multicam and aviators. The grenade reads PUNCH UP. NOT DOWN.
Inspector's note. A treaty with no workable verification regime is normally worthless. This one is the exception: the inspection clause is unenforceable by construction, which makes the treaty self-executing. The Inspectorate has therefore been in continuous compliance since before it was drafted, and intends to remain so by doing nothing.
Specimen 005

Retention Schedule

We classify jokes by how long they survive being repeated at work. Category A: forever. Category B: until Thursday. Category C: it was never funny, you were just tired.
Submitted by Inspectorate of Satire, Records Division
7 Busted a Gut
Base 5 (Laugh)

Specimen 006

Chiasmus

Comedy is tragedy plus time. Tragedy is comedy minus timing.
Submitted by The Management
7 Busted a Gut
Base 5 (Laugh)
  • Originality +1 The first half is inherited. The second half is the contribution, and it is a real one.
  • Unexpected +1 Time and timing are one letter and an entire career apart.

Specimen 007

The Satirical Uncertainty Principle

A flamingo in a tweed jacket holds the J.R.A. Chair of Ironic Studies. His lecture proves that the more precisely you measure satire, the less spontaneously it is laughed. His mug reads I MEASURE, THEREFORE I MAYBE LAUGH. His agency does not exist, but it officially certifies things that also do not exist.
Submitted by Prof. F. L. Amingo, Ph.D.
7 Busted a Gut
Base 4 (Giggle)
  • Visual Aid +1 The flamingo is not decoration. The joke does not survive without the tweed.
  • Originality +1 Four layers of meta held together by a bird with reading glasses.
  • Unexpected +1 The mug. Every time, the mug.
Inspector's note. Certifying nonexistent things through a nonexistent agency is the founding act of this institution. Specimen 007 is therefore both evidence and jurisdiction.
